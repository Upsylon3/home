# HOME artistic direction

HOME should feel like a private, useful digital place: calm, familiar and
consistent. The visual identity is Atari-era warmth with modern readability:
warm brown-black, cream, orange, brass and restrained physical details.

The [design system](docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md) is the practical reference; canonical
assets and tokens live in `design/`. This brief records intent rather than
repeating palettes, component rules and an app-by-app implementation history.

## Principles

- Make the purpose of a screen clear before drawing attention to decoration.
- Use density for working lists and space for important decisions.
- Keep forms, actions and feedback consistent across apps.
- Support light/dark themes and Off/Subtle/Full retro intensity.
- Use motion for state changes, respect reduced motion, and avoid sound by default.
- Keep keyboard access, visible focus, contrast and clear labels.
- Provide useful loading, empty, error and offline states.
- Treat mobile as a real interface, not a smaller desktop screenshot.

## 11. Iconography

Retained section number for existing source comments: use simple, geometric,
consistent icons from the shared family. App marks are distinct within a
common frame. Do not copy another company's logo or trademark.

## Application character

The room metaphor guides tone, not literal decoration: Home is the entrance,
Cloud the archive, Media the gallery, Notes the study, Tasks the desk,
Monitor the utility room and Vault the safe. Vault stays particularly restrained.

Typography, materials and retro behavior are defined once in the
[design system](docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md). Avoid neon, glow, decorative clutter,
generic corporate styling and copied branding. New decoration must have an Off
behavior and must not obscure content or interaction.
